Gubae Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Gubae is an application dedicated to Ethiopian Christian worship, spiritual growth, and education. It provides song lyrics, the Holy Bible, a private prayer journal, church directories, events, and an educational Library.

This policy outlines what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Using Gubae Without an Account (Guest Mode)

You can use most of Gubae completely anonymously without creating an account:

  • You can read lyrics, browse churches and events, study the Bible, and read Library books and articles.
  • In guest mode, no personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • Your bookmarks, reading progress, and local preferences remain strictly stored on your own device.

2. Information Collected When You Sign In

If you choose to sign in with Google so that your data follows you between devices, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used solely to identify your account and authenticate you.
  • Display Name: If provided by Google, used solely to greet you within the app and to show your name where you choose to take part (see section 4).
  • We do not collect your Google password, contacts, photos, or any other personal data from your Google account.

3. Data Saved to Your Cloud Account

To ensure you never lose your spiritual notes when switching devices, the following items are synced to your secure private account:

  • Bookmarked songs, notes, and highlights.
  • Reading plans, reading streaks, and history.
  • Personal prayer journal entries.
  • Follow-up questions asked in the Library.

Your prayers, notes, and readings belong entirely to you. They are never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising profiling.

4. What Other People Can See

Almost everything in Gubae is private to you. These are the exceptions, and they only happen when you choose them:

  • Events: If you mark that you are interested in or attending an event, your display name may be visible to other people looking at that event, and to whoever organises it.
  • Contributions you submit: A song, an event or a church you send in is reviewed by an administrator and, once published, is visible to everyone. Your name is not shown on it.
  • Questions in the Library: A question you ask is read by an administrator so it can be answered. An answer may be published for other readers; your name is not shown with it.
  • The Gubae Family badge: If you are a member, the badge appears on your own profile. The note recording how you supported is never shown to anybody.

Nothing else you do in Gubae is visible to other users. Your prayers, notes, highlights, reading plans and history are yours alone.

5. Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep Gubae free and accessible to all believers, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob:

  • The Holy Bible is 100% Ad-Free: No advertisements ever appear in the Bible reader, verse screens, or scripture search, for anyone.
  • Subscriptions: Gubae does not sell a subscription today. If one is offered in future, subscribers will see no advertisements anywhere in the app.
  • Data Protection: We never share your email, prayers, notes, or reading history with advertisers. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (such as GAID) and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.

6. Financial Contributions & Gubae Family

  • Contributions (via Telebirr, CBE, M-PESA, or Buy Me a Coffee) are handled directly by those payment providers. Gubae never stores or processes card numbers, bank passwords, or PINs, and no payment ever passes through the app.
  • Joining the "Gubae Family" is a voluntary gift supporting the ministry and the app. It shows a member card on your profile; it is not a purchase of ad removal, and family members still see advertisements.

7. Library Books & Intellectual Property

  • Classic Books: All historical books in the Library are in the worldwide Public Domain (published before 1928), free of copyright restrictions.
  • Articles & Teachings: Gathered from Christian ministries under educational fair-use principles, with full attribution to original authors and sources. Gubae claims no ownership over third-party texts.
  • Questions: If you have a question about anything shown in Gubae, email support@getgubae.com.

8. Data Storage, Security & Deletion

  • Security: Data is stored with our cloud infrastructure provider (Supabase), encrypted in transit with industry-standard TLS.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time inside the app: Settings → Your Account → Delete My Account. You may also request deletion by emailing support@getgubae.com.
  • Local Data: Everything kept on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy question or data request:

  • Email: support@getgubae.com
  • Web: https://getgubae.github.io/privacy.html
