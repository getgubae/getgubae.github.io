Gubae Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Gubae is an app for Ethiopian Christian worship: song lyrics, the Bible, a prayer journal, church information and events. It is built to be useful without knowing much about you, and most of it works with no account at all. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Gubae is operated by the Gubae team. You can reach us at support@getgubae.com.

Using Gubae without an account

You can read lyrics, read the Bible, browse churches and events, search, and save favourites without signing in. In that case we do not collect any personal information about you at all. Everything you save stays on your own device.

What we collect if you sign in

You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. When you do, we store:

  • Your email address, which identifies your account.
  • Your display name, if your Google account provides one. It is used to greet you and to address you in the morning prayer reminder.

We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your photos or anything else from your Google account.

What is saved to your account

So that you do not lose your things when you change phone or reinstall the app, the following are stored against your account as well as on your device:

  • Favourite songs
  • Notes and highlights
  • Reading plans and reading history
  • Your prayer journal
  • Achievements and reading streaks

These are yours. They are not shown to other users, they are not sold, and they are not used for advertising.

Things you send us

If you submit a song, an event, a church, a correction, or report an event, that submission is stored with your account so that we can review it and reply to you about it. You can edit or withdraw anything that has not yet been answered, from My Submissions inside the app. Content that is accepted and published becomes part of the Gubae catalogue and is visible to everyone.

If you attach a picture to a submission, it is uploaded to our storage and is publicly readable, because it is intended to be published alongside the event or song.

Advertising

Gubae shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob in order to keep the app free. There is never advertising anywhere in the Bible, for anybody: not in the reader, the chapters, the verses or any scripture screen, and subscribers see no advertising at all. Google may use a device advertising identifier to select and measure ads. Google's own handling of that data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not give Google, or any advertiser, your email address, your prayers, your notes, your reading history or anything else you keep in Gubae.

Supporting Gubae, and the Gubae Family

Gubae is free. If you choose to support it, you can send money through Telebirr, CBE, M-PESA or by card, and those payments are handled entirely by those services. We never see your card details, your bank details or your mobile money PIN, and none of it passes through this app.

After giving, you may send us the email address of your Gubae account so that we can thank you. If you do, an administrator records three things against your account: that you are a member of the Gubae Family, the date you joined, and a short private note of how you supported. That note is for our records only and is never shown to you or to anybody else in the app.

Being in the Gubae Family shows a gold membership card on your profile with your name on it. It does not remove advertising, and it is not a purchase: it is a gift to the ministry. If you save that card to your phone photo gallery, the picture is made on your phone and is not uploaded anywhere.

If paid subscriptions are offered in future, whether your subscription is active and when it expires will also be stored against your account. A subscriber sees no advertising.

What the Library contains, and who owns it

The Library holds historical Christian classic books and educational articles, gathered for personal study and spiritual edification. All classic books are in the worldwide public domain, published before 1928, and carry no copyright restrictions. Articles and teachings are gathered from publicly available ministry sources under educational fair-use principles, and each one names its original author and links to its source. Gubae claims no ownership over third-party texts.

If you hold the copyright in any material and believe it has been included in error, write to copyright@gubae.app and we will verify and remove it promptly.

Questions you ask about Library articles

You can ask a follow-up question about an article in the Library. The question is stored with your account so that we can answer it and show the answer back to you, and it is read by an administrator. Do not put anything in a question that you would not want us to read.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not read your contacts, your photos or your files, except a picture you deliberately choose to attach to a submission.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from what you read, pray or listen to.

Where your information is kept

Account information, submissions and the things saved to your account are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, on servers outside Ethiopia. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Gubae account from inside the app: Settings → Your account → Delete my account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your sign-in details, your email address, your favourites, notes, highlights, reading plans, prayers, reading history and achievements. Submissions you sent that have already been published as part of the catalogue remain published, but are no longer linked to you.

You can also ask us to delete your account by writing to support@getgubae.com from the address you signed up with. We will action it within 30 days.

Anything kept only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Gubae is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Gubae handles your information we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
