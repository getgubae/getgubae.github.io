Delete your Gubae account

Application: Gubae : ግባኤ — com.gubae.app

You can delete your Gubae account at any time, and you do not need anybody's permission to do it. This page explains how, and exactly what goes and what stays.

1. Deleting from inside the app

This is the quickest way, and it happens immediately.

  1. Open Gubae and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete my account.
  4. Read what it tells you, then type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is removed there and then, and you are signed out. Nobody can undo this, including us.

2. Asking us to delete it instead

If you cannot open the app — a lost phone, or an account you can no longer sign in to — write to us:

  • Email: support@getgubae.com
  • Send it from the email address of the account, so we can tell it is yours. If you cannot, tell us which address the account uses and we will write back to that address to confirm before anything is deleted.
  • Put Delete my account in the subject.

We will confirm when it is done. We do not ask for a reason, and you do not have to give one.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account itself, with your email address and display name.
  • Bookmarked songs, your notes and your highlights.
  • Reading plans, reading days, reading history and streaks.
  • Library bookmarks and any questions you asked.
  • Events you marked an interest in.
  • Anything you submitted that is still waiting to be reviewed.
  • The copy of all of this held on your phone.

4. What stays, and why

  • Contributions that were already published. A song, an event or a church of yours that an administrator approved stays in Gubae, because other people are using that catalogue. Your name is removed from it, so it is no longer connected to you. Your name was never shown on it in the first place.
  • Backup copies. Our hosting provider keeps routine backups of the database. A copy of your rows can remain in those for a short period until the backups age out and are overwritten. Nobody reads them, and they are not used for anything.

5. Your prayer journal

Your prayers are kept on your phone only. They are never sent to us, there is no copy of them on any server, and no administrator has ever been able to read them.

That means deleting your account does not need to reach them. Deleting your account clears them from the app along with everything else, and uninstalling Gubae removes anything left.

It also means that if you change phones, your prayers do not follow you unless your phone's own backup carries them.

6. How long it takes

  • From inside the app: immediately.
  • By email: within 30 days of us confirming the request is yours, and usually much sooner.

7. Questions

  • Email: support@getgubae.com
  • Privacy policy: https://getgubae.github.io/privacy.html
